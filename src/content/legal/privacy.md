mamasays is a daily check-in and memory catcher for busy moms. What you share here is personal: how full your tank is, what you're setting down, the moments you never want to forget. We treat it that way. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have.

mamasays is operated by [mamasays, Inc.], a Delaware corporation ("mamasays," "we," "us"). This policy covers the mamasays app, mamasaystoday.com, our emails, and anywhere else that links to this policy (together, the "Service"). Questions go to <privacy@mamasaystoday.com>.

## The short version

- **Your entries are yours.** Check-ins, memories, photos and notes are private to you. We use them to run mamasays for you.
- **We never sell your information.** Not to advertisers, data brokers, brand partners or anyone else.
- **What you write never goes to analytics.** Our activity records hold option IDs and counts, never your words or photos.
- **Research is optional and anonymous.** It's off until you turn it on.
- **You're in control.** Download or delete your data anytime.
- **mamasays isn't medical care.** If you're struggling, see section 13.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

- Your email address, and your first name if you give it, when you sign up on mamasaystoday.com. To sign in to the app: [your phone number, if you use a text-message sign-in link, or the name and email Apple or Google shares when you sign in with them]. We don't use passwords.
- How you found us: the referring site and campaign tags in the link you clicked (for example, an Instagram post).
- Your settings: which check-ins you want, how often, your nudge times and your time zone.

### Your Setup Answers

What you tell us when you set up the app: how many kids you have and their age ranges (including expecting), how work and life outside work feel right now, what your world is made of these days, what's important to you right now, how you'd describe this season and when you think it might shift, a mantra, a priority for this season, and any "go-to" items you add.

### User Content

- **Morning check-ins:** how much is in your tank, where your focus and best energy go, what you're protecting, what you can set down, and an optional word for the day.
- **Evening check-ins:** how the day felt and what was hard, your highlight, how your best-energy plans went, what you deserve credit for, what you're grateful for, and any note to tomorrow.
- **Middle-of-the-night check-ins:** what's got you up and how you're holding up, both optional.
- **Mental load dump:** the things you list and how you sort them.
- **Core memories:** moments you save, with an optional photo. These can include your kids or other people.
- **Notes to other moms:** encouragement you choose to leave at night (section 7).

Most check-in answers are taps on preset options. Anything you type yourself, and every photo, is treated as private content: it's encrypted, never sent to analytics, and never used in research.

### Error and Diagnostic Data

- **App activity:** when you start, finish or skip a check-in and how long it took, which screens you open, and which nudges were sent and opened. These records carry option IDs and counts, never what you typed.
- **Device information:** device type, operating system, app version, IP address and crash reports.
- **Push token:** so we can send the nudges you turn on.

### Feedback and Messages

Support emails, comments and beta feedback forms, including any questions about what you'd pay for mamasays.

**What we don't collect:** your precise location, your contacts, your photo library (only the photos you pick), or biometric data.

## 2. How We Use Information

- **To run mamasays for you:** save your check-ins and memories, build your day card, Sunday week in review and trends, and send the nudges you set.
- **To fit it to you:** show options that match your life (for example, no partner or kid options if they don't apply), and pick the kind words and mantras you see.
- **To support you:** answer questions and fix problems.
- **To improve mamasays:** learn which features help, using app activity and option IDs, not your words.
- **To keep things safe:** review notes to other moms, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms.
- **To email you:** a sign-up confirmation, your beta invite, your Sunday recap if you turn it on, service messages and feedback requests. Every non-essential email has a one-click unsubscribe.
- **To meet legal obligations.**

We'll ask before using your information in a way this policy doesn't describe.

## 3. Data Storage and Infrastructure

- **Website:** mamasaystoday.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics (section 8). Sign-up emails and first names go to Customer.io, which sends our emails.
- **App:** your account, setup answers and check-ins are stored in a database hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in a U.S. region. Photos are stored in private Amazon S3 storage in the same U.S. region. [If we integrate with Google Photos instead, update this and section 9.]
- **On your phone:** the app keeps a copy of your entries and photos on your device so it works without a connection, and syncs them to your account.
- **Location:** all of it is stored and processed in the United States.

## 4. Encryption and Security

- Everything moving between your device, our website and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
- What you type and every photo are encrypted at rest. Photos are opened only through short-lived private links, never public ones.
- We don't read what you type or look at your photos. Our team accesses them only for system operations (finding and fixing errors and keeping mamasays secure), when you ask us to for support, or when the law requires it.
- Your tap answers (like your tank level or what you're setting down) can be combined across users so we can see what's working. That happens under a random ID, never tied to your name or email, and we only look at groups large enough that no one can be picked out.
- Nudges never show your own words or how full your tank is on your lock screen. They say things like "Your week in review is ready."
- Access to our systems is limited to people who need it and protected by multi-factor authentication.
- No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you and any regulators the law requires, including the FTC under its Health Breach Notification Rule where it applies.

## 5. Health-Related Reflections

Some of what you share can say something about your physical or mental health: how full your tank is, what felt hard, why you're up at night, how you're holding up. Some state laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act, call this "consumer health data," even for apps outside HIPAA. We give it that level of care for everyone, wherever you live.

- **We ask first.** Before your first check-in, we ask for your consent to collect and use this information to run and improve mamasays, including combining your tap answers with other users' answers, under a random ID, to see what's working. It's its own clear choice, not buried in our Terms.
- **Only for you.** We use it to show your day card, week in review and trends, and to keep mamasays working and safe.
- **Not shared without separate consent,** except with the services in section 9 that store it for us.
- **Never sold, never used for ads.**
- **You can take it back.** Withdraw consent by deleting your check-ins or your account. We stop collecting it and delete what we hold (section 12).

## 6. Research About Modern Motherhood

Part of our mission is to help the world see what modern motherhood is really like. If you turn on research sharing, we may count your tap answers (like tank levels, what you're setting down, or why you're up at night) toward insights we publish, such as "most moms say their best energy goes to work."

- **Off until you say yes.** It's separate from agreeing to use the app, and you can change it anytime in Settings. Your experience is the same either way.
- **Combined and anonymous.** We only report on groups of at least [50] moms, with names, emails and other identifiers removed.
- **Never your words, photos or memories.** We'll never quote you unless you specifically agree.
- **No re-identifying.** We won't try to re-identify anyone, and we require the same of anyone we share insights with.

## 10. Children's Privacy

mamasays is for adults 18 and older and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn a child has an account, we'll delete it, and a parent can ask us to by emailing <privacy@mamasaystoday.com>.

**Your kids' information.** You'll probably share things about your kids: their ages, their milestones, their photos. That stays private to your account. We don't use it for marketing, share it with partners, or include it in research, and your photos are never used in our marketing.

## 12. Account Deletion and Data Control

**Deleting your account.** Delete it in Settings or by emailing us. Within [30 days] we delete your account, everything you entered, your photos and your push tokens, tell the services in section 9 to do the same, remove you from our email lists, and email you a confirmation. App activity records are kept only under a random ID cut off from your account. We keep security logs (sign-ins, account changes and IP addresses, never what you wrote) for up to [13 months] so we can investigate misuse, and anything else the law requires.

**Your rights.** Wherever you live, you can:

- See and download your information
- Correct anything that's wrong
- Delete your information or your account
- Withdraw consent for health-related reflections or research sharing
- Opt out of non-essential emails
- Appeal if we turn down a request

Use Settings or email <privacy@mamasaystoday.com>. We'll verify it's you and respond within 45 days. You can use an authorized agent where your state allows. We'll never treat you differently for using these rights.

**State notices.** Residents of California, Washington, Nevada, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and other states with privacy laws have these rights under state law. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general.

## 13. mamasays Is Not Medical Care

mamasays is a reflection practice. It isn't therapy, medical advice or a crisis service, and no one watches check-ins in real time. If you're struggling, you don't have to carry it alone: call or text the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262), any time. In a crisis, call or text 988. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.

## 14. International Users

mamasays is built for people in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the U.S. If you use it from somewhere else, your information is transferred to the U.S. and handled under this policy.
